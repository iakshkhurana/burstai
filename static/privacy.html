PRIVACY NOTICE

What happens to your text

Last updated 22 November 2026

The short version

  • Your essay is not stored. It is processed in memory for the scan and then discarded.
  • Checking it requires sending parts of it to other services (listed below). There is no way to search the web for copied text without doing that.
  • Accounts are optional and hold your email, a password hash and the scores of scans you save. Never the essay.
  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics cookies. Your data is not sold or used to train anything.
  • You can clear this device or delete your account at any time from My writing.

1. Where your text goes during a scan

Your browser sends the text over HTTPS to this site, which passes it to the BurstAI checking engine. The engine keeps it in memory only for the duration of the scan and does not write it to disk or to logs. To do the checks, the engine contacts these third parties:

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Web search engines (DuckDuckGo and others, via an open-source metasearch library)Up to about 20 short phrases (8 to 12 words each) taken from your textTo find pages that contain the same wording
Wikipedia, Europe PMC, CrossrefA handful of keywords from those phrasesTo find encyclopedia articles and paper abstracts on the same topic
LanguageTool (public grammar service)The text itself, up to the first 20,000 charactersSpelling and grammar suggestions. Their own privacy policy applies.
Google GeminiThe text, up to the first 12,000 characters. Only if you add your own Gemini key.Context-aware review of grammar suggestions and a second opinion on AI likelihood. Google's terms for your key apply; on Google's free tier, submitted content may be used to improve Google's products.
Websites found by the searchNothing from you. The engine downloads those public pages to compare them with your text.Sentence-by-sentence matching

If you do not want your text sent to any of these, do not scan it here. BurstAI is open source, so you can also run it on your own computer, where the language models work offline and you control which services are contacted.

2. What is stored, and where

In your browser

  • The text and result of your latest scan, until you close the tab (session storage).
  • The titles and scores of your recent scans (local storage), so the home page and My writing can show your history.
  • Your Gemini key, if you add one (local storage). It is sent with your scans and is never stored on our side.

Use My writing → Clear history on this device to remove all of it. Do this on shared computers.

If you create an account

  • Your email address, display name, and a salted PBKDF2 hash of your password (the password itself is never stored).
  • For each saved scan: title, date, word count, scores, the list of matched source links, and per-sentence flags without the sentence text.
  • Documents you upload to your private document bank, in full, until you remove them. They are compared only against your own scans and are visible only to you.

Signed reports

When you choose "Create signed copy", the scores, source links, a title, the date and a one-way fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of the text are saved so that anyone with the link can verify them. The fingerprint cannot be turned back into the text. Signed copies contain no essay text; to have one removed, contact us with its report ID.

Operational data

  • To prevent abuse we count requests per visitor using a salted hash of the IP address, kept for at most one hour.
  • The engine caches search results and downloaded public web pages for up to seven days to stay fast. The cache is keyed by a hash and holds no essay text.
  • The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes IP addresses and standard request metadata to deliver and protect it.

3. Cookies

One strictly necessary cookie, burstai_session, is set when you log in so you stay signed in. It is HttpOnly and expires after 30 days or when you log out. There are no advertising, analytics or third-party cookies, and fonts are served from this site rather than a font network.

4. Your choices

  • Delete your account and everything stored for it: My writing → Delete my account. It takes effect immediately.
  • Clear this device: My writing → Clear history on this device.
  • Use it without an account. Everything works logged out.
  • Leave out Gemini. If you never add a key, nothing is sent to Google.

5. Students and schools

If you are a minor, use BurstAI with the permission of a parent, guardian or teacher. If you check someone else's writing, make sure you are allowed to, and read how to use results responsibly.

6. Who runs this and how to reach us

BurstAI is an open-source project operated by its maintainer. For privacy questions, removal requests or to report a security problem, open an issue or use the contact details at github.com/iakshkhurana/burstai. Please do not post personal data or essay text in a public issue.

7. Changes

If what we do with data changes, this page changes first and the date at the top is updated.

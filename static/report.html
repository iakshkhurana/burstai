How these scores are made. Similarity compares word sequences and sentence meaning against public web pages and Wikipedia (plus any documents you added to your local corpus); private archives of student papers are not searched. AI likelihood combines two language models' predictability (Binoculars), sentence-level burstiness and stylometric signals. Scores are probabilities and can be wrong — use them to start a conversation, never as the only proof.

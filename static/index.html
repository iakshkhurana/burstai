How BurstAI checks a paper

  1. 1

    Fingerprint the text

    Overlapping word sequences are hashed and searched across the web and open academic indexes; a meaning-based pass catches paraphrase.

  2. 2

    Read the rhythm

    Each sentence is scored for predictability and burstiness by a pair of language models, then an ensemble gives an AI-likelihood with reasons.

  3. 3

    Show the evidence

    You get the matched source side by side, the flagged sentences and fixes — so a person makes the final call, not a number.

Questions, answered

Is BurstAI’s plagiarism checker really free? +

Yes. Plagiarism, AI detection, grammar and citations are free with no word cap and no credit card. It runs on your own machine; nothing is sent to a paid vendor.

How accurate is the AI detector? +

No AI detector is 100% accurate, including paid ones. BurstAI shows sentence-level reasons so you can judge each flag instead of trusting a single score.

Can it detect ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude text? +

It looks for patterns common to large language models in general, so it covers text from all major AI writers.

Do you store or resell my essay? +

No. Your text is processed for the scan and not added to any database unless you choose to add it to your local corpus.

Is BurstAI the same as Turnitin? +

No. Turnitin also searches its private archive of past student papers, which no outside tool can access. BurstAI checks the open web and open academic sources, plus any documents you add yourself.
